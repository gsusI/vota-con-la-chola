# Code of conduct

This project handles contested public evidence. Participation must remain
factual, respectful, and safe for reviewers, public officials, contributors,
and people represented in source data.

## Expected behavior

- Challenge claims with evidence, not personal attacks.
- Separate verified fact, inference, uncertainty, and opinion.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when reviewing a claim or source.
- Protect credentials, private contact details, and unnecessary personal data.
- Accept corrections publicly and preserve an auditable correction trail.
- Help newcomers reproduce results without ridicule or gatekeeping.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory language, or coordinated abuse.
- Publishing private data that is unnecessary for public accountability.
- Calling a person corrupt, criminal, or unethical without the publication gate
  defined in `docs/method/integrity-signal-policy.md`.
- Fabricating, selectively editing, or concealing evidence.
- Using project channels for partisan mobilization unrelated to evidence review.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`. Do not
post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers may warn, remove content, restrict
participation, or ban an account. Enforcement decisions must record the rule,
evidence, action, and appeal route while minimizing personal data.

Good-faith disagreement about methods, sources, or conclusions is welcome.
Retaliation against a reporter, reviewer, or correction requester is not.
